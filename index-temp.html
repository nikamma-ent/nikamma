follow us

nikamma started in 2025, born out of a shared love for music and a desire to do things a little differently.

We didn't start out as industry professionals or seasoned business people. We were a bunch of creative folks brought together by a shared love for music, figuring things out as we went. Much of what we know, we've learnt along the way, and we're still learning.

Today, we work across the many ways music and ideas find their way into the world. From artist development and label services to live experiences and creative projects, our work is driven by a curiosity for what music can be, where it can go and how it can connect with people.

Here's to the good-for-nothings.
