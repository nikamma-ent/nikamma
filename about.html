ni·kam·ma (Hindi, adj.) good-for-nothing. We took the name anyway.

nikamma started in 2025, born out of a shared love for music and a desire to do things a little differently.

Its founder, Mehul Gupta, was working as a data scientist in the Bay Area when he decided to leave it behind and move back home to satiate a long-standing creative itch. What started as an instinct to be closer to music and the people making it slowly grew into something of its own.

We didn't start out as industry professionals or seasoned business people. We were a bunch of creative folks brought together by a shared love for music, figuring things out as we went. Much of what we know, we've learnt along the way, and we're still learning.

Today, we work across the many ways music and ideas find their way into the world. From artist development and label services to live experiences and creative projects, our work is driven by a curiosity for what music can be, where it can go and how it can connect with people.

Sometimes we're building careers. Sometimes we're putting music out into the world. Sometimes we're building a space for it to exist. And sometimes we're figuring out how an idea can become something bigger than it was to begin with.

We're interested in looking beyond the obvious. Beyond numbers, algorithms and fleeting attention. In building things with intent, finding new ways for music to reach people and creating experiences that bring people together.

And none of it means much without the people on the other side. The ones who listen, discover, show up, buy tickets, share music with friends and make room for independent voices.

We're still figuring things out. Still learning, experimenting and finding our way.

Here's to the good-for-nothings.
